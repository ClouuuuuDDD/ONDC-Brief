01Strategic Diagnosis

The core paradox nobody is solving.

You cannot build brand loyalty for a protocol.
You build it for outcomes.
Market the promise, not the plumbing.

SELLER FEAR //
Will there be buyers?

Supply is growing fast. Demand activation lags. The chicken-and-egg is half-solved and nobody is closing the gap with a marketing strategy.

BUYER FEAR //
What if delivery fails?

Unbundled logistics creates a weakest-link problem. Any bad experience with any partner poisons the entire network's reputation. No central authority to fix it.

BOTH FEAR //
Who do I complain to?

Dispute resolution is fragmented. Trust-sensitive buyers see this as a dealbreaker before they even attempt a first order.

The Primary Wedge

Restaurants. Highest-frequency. Most commission-angry. A double-sided win: sellers save 15-20 margin points, buyers pay less for delivery. Stories write themselves.

03Core Insights

7 things nobody is saying loudly enough.

01 //
Supply is ahead of demand in Tier 2/3. Demand is ahead of supply in metros.
84% of sellers are small businesses concentrated in smaller cities. Buyer apps skew metro. Geographic mismatch is slowing the network effect. Fix this before anything else.
Geographic
02 //
Interoperability is ONDC's biggest asset and its biggest UX liability.
A buyer on Paytm, a seller on Vikra, fulfilled by Shadowfax. Any weak link breaks trust. ONDC cannot unilaterally optimize the experience. It must enforce SLA standards across every layer.
Structural
03 //
Growth was subsidy-driven. Retention requires organic value.
Post-subsidy, retail order volumes dropped. Price alone does not create habit. UX consistency and reliability must follow the initial incentive. Discounts are a loan, not a foundation.
Retention Risk
04 //
Buyer app fragmentation is killing brand recall.
Consumers ordering via Magicpin have no idea they are on ONDC. A universal "Powered by ONDC" badge with a consistent trust promise can create network-level brand equity without disrupting the app layer.
Branding Gap
05
B2B wholesale is the hidden flywheel. If a kirana's first ONDC experience is buying inventory cheaper, she becomes a seller-side advocate automatically. Solve credit, unlock everything.
06 //
Fashion and D2C growth is real but fragile. Trust, not discovery, is the bottleneck.
Return rates and quality complaints will stall growth unless ONDC introduces a network-wide verified seller layer. A badge system modelled on FSSAI certification would do this at scale.
Category Risk
07 //
The smallest repeatable unit is pincode density, not city launch.
Target 50+ active sellers and 500+ monthly orders in one pincode before expanding. Network effects compound locally first. Launching in "600 cities" means nothing if none have critical density.

Six campaigns that could actually move the needle.

P1

06Rollout Plan

Week 1. Month 1. Quarter 1.

Week 1 // Diagnose and Arm
Build assets. Find stories. Spend nothing on media.
  • Audit 10 most-active seller cities. Talk to 100 sellers. Find 5 with dramatic margin improvement. Record unpolished on phone cameras.
  • Build Commission Calculator tool, restaurant and retail versions. Deploy via WhatsApp bot.
  • Co-develop one joint message with top 3 buyer apps by MAU.
  • Activate ONDC Mitra pilot in 3 Tier 2 cities via CSC center network.
  • Identify 30 micro-influencers doing food and price content in Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Telugu.
Month 1 // Light the Match
Activate supply side. Acquire first buyers at near-zero CAC.
  • Launch Kitna Diya on WhatsApp and YouTube. 10 real restaurant videos, authentic, subtitled.
  • Run Namma Yatri cross-sell in Bengaluru and Hyderabad. Target 50,000 new retail buyers from existing ride-hailing base.
  • Seed 90-second DSC-style video via 30 micro-influencers. No paid distribution. WhatsApp only.
  • Distribute ONDC Dukaan Badge stickers to 10,000 sellers across 5 cities. Document rollout visually.
  • Begin RWA WhatsApp seeding in 50 housing societies in Delhi NCR, Pune, Hyderabad.
Quarter 1 // Build the Flywheel
Scale what worked. Launch the Diwali Bazaar. Build B2B proof.
  • Pre-heat ONDC Diwali Bazaar in Month 2. Give early joiners "founding seller" status. Recruit sellers 60 days before Diwali.
  • Roll ONDC Mitra program to all districts with CSC presence. Target 500,000 potential Mitras nationally.
  • Partner with 2 state governments for ONDC procurement at ration shops and municipal supplies.
  • Publish first ONDC Seller Income Report. Real data, margin savings, cities. Make it press-worthy not a PR release.
  • Start B2B kirana pilot: 10,000 kiranas buy wholesale via ONDC B2B in 3 states. Measure savings for next cycle.

07What ONDC Must Not Do

6 traps. Avoid every one.

01
Do not become a consumer brand directly.ONDC is not Amazon. Consumer campaigns that position ONDC as a shopping destination will confuse. Market outcomes. Let buyer apps own the destination.
02
Do not obsess over metro cities.70%+ of demand already comes from Tier 2 and 3. The next 100 million buyers are in Kanpur, not Connaught Place. Budget accordingly.
03
Do not compete on discounts.Amazon has deeper pockets. You will lose a cashback war. Compete on fairness, locality, and trust. That terrain is where ONDC wins and Amazon cannot follow.
04
Do not hide the government backing.For Tier 2 and 3 trust-sensitive buyers, "sarkar ka platform" is a feature, not a liability. Lean into it. It is an advantage money cannot buy.
05
Do not try to solve every category at once.One wedge category per city. Food in metros. Grocery in Tier 2. Artisan products in craft clusters. Density beats coverage at every stage of growth.
06
Do not let buyer app fragmentation kill network recall.If consumers do not know they are on ONDC, the network builds no equity. A universal "Powered by ONDC" badge is the minimum viable brand layer across all apps.
The single best thesis

ONDC wins not by building a better Amazon, but by becoming the economic backbone that lets 50 million Indian sellers keep the money they have been paying as tribute to foreign platforms.
